Common questions about our podiatry services in Astoria, Queens, NY.

What is diabetic foot care and why is it important?

Diabetic foot care involves regular examinations, monitoring of circulation and nerve function, wound care and preventative treatment specifically designed for patients with diabetes. Diabetes can reduce circulation and sensation in the feet, making even minor injuries potentially serious. Regular podiatric care can prevent complications, infections and in severe cases, amputations.

What are custom orthotics and do I need them?

Custom orthotics are shoe inserts specifically made from a mold of your foot to correct biomechanical issues, relieve pain and improve alignment. Unlike over-the-counter insoles, custom orthotics are prescribed by our podiatrists after a full assessment of your foot structure and gait. They are particularly beneficial for patients with flat feet, high arches, heel pain, bunions or sports-related foot conditions.

Do you treat children's foot problems?

Yes, we treat patients of all ages including infants, children and teenagers. Common pediatric foot conditions we address include flat feet, in-toeing, growing pains, ingrown toenails and sports injuries. Early diagnosis and treatment of foot problems in children can prevent more serious issues as they grow, so we encourage parents to bring children in for a foot assessment whenever they notice walking difficulties, pain or unusual gait patterns.

Heel Pain & Plantar Fasciitis

Heel pain is one of the most common conditions we treat at Ideal Podiatry. Service 02

Bunions & Ingrown Toenails

Bunions and ingrown toenails are two of the most frequently treated conditions in our Queens podiatry practice. A bunion is a bony bump that forms at the base of the big toe, causing pain, swelling and difficulty wearing shoes. Our podiatrists offer both conservative management — including custom orthotics, padding and footwear advice — and surgical correction for severe cases. Ingrown toenails can be extremely painful and prone to infection. We provide safe, quick and effective in-office treatment to remove the ingrown portion and, when recurrence is a problem, a minor procedure to prevent it from growing back. Both conditions are treated with precision and care for every patient we see.

Service 03

Diabetic Foot Care

For patients with diabetes, regular foot care is not just a convenience — it is a critical part of their overall health management. Diabetes affects circulation and nerve function in the feet, which means small injuries or infections can escalate quickly if left untreated. At Ideal Podiatry, our diabetic foot care program includes comprehensive foot exams, assessment of vascular and neurological function, wound care management, nail and skin care, and education on proper foot hygiene and protective footwear. We work closely with each patient to develop a preventative care routine that minimizes the risk of ulcers, infections and complications throughout the year in Queens, NY.

Service 04

Custom Orthotics

Custom orthotics are one of the most effective tools in podiatric medicine for treating and preventing a wide range of foot and lower limb problems. Unlike generic shoe inserts, our custom orthotics are fabricated from a precise mold of your individual foot and prescribed based on a thorough biomechanical assessment by our Queens podiatrists. They are designed to correct your specific structural issues, redistribute pressure, reduce strain on tendons and joints, and improve your overall gait. Custom orthotics are beneficial for plantar fasciitis, bunions, flat feet, high arches, knee pain, back pain and sports performance. We fit patients of all ages and foot types throughout Astoria, Queens and surrounding areas.
